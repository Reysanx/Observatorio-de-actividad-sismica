# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

An operator monitoring recent earthquakes who needs to inspect magnitude, depth, feed status, and related metrics and data.

## Product Purpose

The Observatorio presents global earthquake activity from its live feed and an isolated replay view so an operator can detect relevant activity and quickly verify feed status and the selected time interval.

## Positioning

No competitive or commercial positioning has been confirmed. The current product combines a map, shared filters, ingestion/feed status, event analytics, and separate live and replay data scopes.

## Operating Context

The operator reviews event time in UTC, magnitude, depth, location, connection and ingestion status, filtered distributions, hourly activity, historical comparison, and event rows. Live and replay data are presented as distinct scopes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve the current live/replay scopes, shared filters, map, analytics, table, sorting, pagination, event selection, alerts, empty states, and attribution.
- A visual redesign may change HTML presentation and CSS only. Do not edit TypeScript, services, application logic, Angular bindings, structural directives, or event handlers.
- Do not alter the tagged ingestion evidence JSON.

## Brand Commitments

- The user requires a professional dark presentation with high contrast and high data density.
- All current content and functional behavior must remain intact.

## Evidence on Hand

- The application identifies the live seismic data as USGS data.
- Existing attribution references Natural Earth and OpenStreetMap.
- No customer testimonials, commercial claims, or performance claims have been confirmed.
